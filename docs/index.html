Scan limitations and important safety notes

Some tokens or old approvals may be omitted when indexer history or RPC data is incomplete. Not finding a token does not prove it is absent.

Security methodology and independent assurance

GUARDIAN 360 provides automated, limited-scope read-only observations. No independent security audit has been completed or certified for this release. There is no wallet safety guarantee. Scan findings are incomplete unless the evidence proves otherwise; never interact with unsolicited tokens or approve a transaction based solely on a ticker.

An external review, when completed, will be published here with auditor identity, commit hash, scope, date and report link. No audit seal is displayed until independently verifiable.
